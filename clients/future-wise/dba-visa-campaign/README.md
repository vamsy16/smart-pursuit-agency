## ⚡ The 60-second version

**The hook:** *"H-1B not picked? On OPT, clock running? Don't pay any university a dollar yet."*

**The turn:** August 12, 2026 — SEVP tightened CPT scrutiny. Practical training has to be an integral part of your curriculum, not a job permit with a tuition bill.

**The value:** Three questions to ask before enrolling. (1) Is the practicum required for every student? (2) Is it tied to your major? (3) Will the school put its CPT policy in writing?

**The CTA:** Comment "DBA" → automated DM within 30 seconds → 3-question qualifier → WhatsApp → 15-minute compliance review.

---

## 🎯 Why this converts (the strategy in four lines)

1. **It sells verification, not the degree.** Everyone else is selling a university. You're selling a checklist. Checklists get filled in — degrees get "thought about".
2. **It's the only honest voice in a category full of guarantees.** The line *"your school authorizes CPT, not us"* is the most credible sentence in the feed, and it's what makes a nervous prospect hand over their phone number.
3. **One word, one comment, zero friction.** "Comment DBA" removes every step between impulse and lead. No "link in bio" detour.
4. **Everything is trackable.** Hook rate → CTR → qualified lead → application. The script exists to be killed and replaced weekly until the winner emerges.

## ⚠️ The one thing that will break this campaign

Not the creative. Not the budget. **A promise you can't keep.**

Never guarantee CPT approval, visa outcomes, admission, or status. Say what you control — a free checklist, a verified comparison, a real intake date — and let the honesty be the differentiator. In a market this anxious and this expensive, credibility *is* the conversion mechanism.

SunVolt Solar is a **fictional demonstration brand**. All photography on this
demonstration site is **representative imagery** sourced from third parties. It is
**not** presented as SunVolt Solar's own projects, and SunVolt Solar does not claim
ownership of any photograph used here.

## Sources

Images were sourced from publicly available, legally usable photography pools —
primarily **Wikimedia Commons** and **Pexels**. They were resized, compressed and
converted to WebP/JPEG derivatives for web delivery. No image was altered in a way

## Before any real-world use

This is important and not optional:

1. **Re-verify the licence of every image** against its original source page, and
   record the photographer credit and licence type (e.g. CC BY-SA 4.0, Pexels licence).
2. **Add attribution** wherever the licence requires it — several Wikimedia Commons
   images carry share-alike/attribution conditions.
3. **Replace with the client's own photography** at the earliest opportunity. For a
   real solar installer, photographs of their own crews, their own installations and
   their own completed roofs will always outperform stock imagery — and remove the
   licensing question entirely.
4. **Never label third-party photography as the client's completed projects.** The
   gallery on this demo is explicitly captioned "Representative Solar installation
   examples" for exactly this reason.

## Processing applied

- Resized to 480 / 960 / 1600 px widths
- WebP at quality 82, plus a 1600 px JPEG fallback
- EXIF metadata stripped
- Auto-oriented
- Served via `srcset` / `sizes` with `loading="lazy"` below the fold

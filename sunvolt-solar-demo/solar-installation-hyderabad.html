Local context

Rooftop solar in Hyderabad

Hyderabad gets strong, consistent sunlight for most of the year, which makes rooftop generation practical across much of the city. The variables that actually decide a project here are local ones.

  • Roof type varies widely — flat RCC roofs are common on independent houses and need a raised mounting structure, while sheet roofs on commercial units need a different approach.
  • Dust matters — accumulation reduces generation, so practical roof access for cleaning is worth planning at design stage rather than after handover.
  • Shading from neighbouring buildings — in denser localities, an adjacent structure or overhead tank can affect a section of the array at certain hours.
  • Approvals are local — the applicable application, inspection and metering process depends on your connection type and the relevant authority.

Questions

Hyderabad solar FAQs

Do you install across all of Hyderabad?
We cover Hyderabad and Secunderabad along with the Rangareddy, Medchal and Sangareddy districts. Whether a specific address can be serviced is confirmed at enquiry stage — we would rather say no early than waste your time.
How much does solar installation cost in Hyderabad?
Cost depends on system size, equipment selection, mounting structure and roof conditions. Flat RCC roofs typically need a raised structure, which differs from a sheet-roof installation. A meaningful figure follows the assessment.
How much generation can I expect here?
Generation depends on your system size, roof orientation, shading and how clean the panels are kept. Dust accumulation is a real factor in this region. An assessment gives an indicative range for your specific site rather than a generic figure.
Will a flat RCC roof work for solar?
Usually yes. Flat roofs are common on independent houses here and are fitted with a raised mounting structure that sets panels at an appropriate tilt. The structural condition of the roof and available shade-free area are checked during the site assessment.
How long does installation take?
The physical installation of a typical residential system is usually a matter of days once materials are on site. The overall timeline includes assessment, design, procurement and the applicable approval and metering process.
Do you handle approvals and net metering?
The applicable application, inspection and metering process forms part of the installation scope. Requirements and timelines are set by the relevant authority and vary by connection type.
Are government incentives available?
Eligibility and incentive availability depend on applicable government policies, regulations, location and system requirements. Confirm current eligibility before making a purchase decision.

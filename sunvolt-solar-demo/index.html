Why solar

Why consider rooftop solar?

Solar suits some properties better than others. These are the reasons it is usually worth assessing — each one depends on your usage, your roof and your site conditions.

Lower electricity dependence

Generating part of your own electricity reduces how much you draw from the grid during daylight hours, depending on usage and system size.

Long-term savings potential

A solar system is a long-life asset. Potential savings build over years and depend on consumption, tariff structure and generation at your site.

Better control over energy costs

Producing part of your own power makes a portion of your monthly expense more predictable, subject to applicable regulations and tariffs.

Use of unused rooftop space

Most rooftops sit empty. A solar array puts that area to work without affecting how the rest of the building is used.

Renewable energy generation

Electricity generated on site is renewable and produced where it is consumed, reducing transmission losses.

Potential eligibility for incentives

Certain rooftop solar installations may be eligible for applicable incentives. Eligibility depends on current government policy, location and system requirements, and should be confirmed before purchase.

Solar performance varies by property. Generation, savings and payback depend on electricity usage, tariff structure, roof orientation, shading, equipment selected and applicable regulations. Nothing on this page is a guaranteed outcome.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a rooftop solar system cost?
Cost depends on system size, panel and inverter selection, mounting structure, roof conditions and the scope of work included. Because these vary from property to property, a meaningful figure can only be given after an assessment. Be cautious of any price quoted before someone has looked at your usage and your roof.
How many solar panels do I need?
The number of panels follows the system size, and the system size follows your monthly electricity consumption and usable roof area. A useful starting point is your average monthly units. From there, panel count depends on the wattage of the modules selected.
Can solar reduce my electricity bill?
Solar can reduce the amount of electricity you draw from the grid, which may reduce your bill. How much depends on your consumption pattern, how much of your usage happens during daylight hours, your tariff structure, system size and actual generation. We do not offer guaranteed savings figures.
Is rooftop solar suitable for every house?
No. Suitability depends on usable shade-free roof area, roof structure and condition, orientation, access and whether you have the right to install on the roof. Apartment rooftops usually involve shared ownership, which changes what is possible. An assessment establishes this before anything else.
How long does solar installation take?
The physical installation of a typical residential system is usually completed in a matter of days once materials are on site. The overall timeline is generally longer, because it includes assessment, design, procurement and the applicable approval and metering process, which varies by location and connection type.
What happens during a power cut?
A standard on-grid system shuts down during a grid outage as a safety requirement, so it does not supply power during a power cut. If backup during outages is important to you, a hybrid system with battery storage is the option to discuss — it costs more and should be sized deliberately.
What maintenance does solar require?
Day-to-day maintenance is mainly keeping the panels clean, since dust reduces generation. Beyond that, periodic inspection of wiring, mounting and inverter performance is recommended. Practical roof access matters more than most people expect, so it is worth considering at design stage.
Can businesses install rooftop solar?
Yes. Offices, retail units, warehouses, schools and small industrial premises commonly consider rooftop solar. Commercial systems are usually sized against daytime consumption and sanctioned load, and the approval process differs from residential connections.
Is financing available?
Financing options for rooftop solar exist through various lenders and schemes, and availability and terms change over time. We can discuss what options may apply to your situation, but any financing arrangement is subject to the lender's own eligibility criteria and approval.
Are government incentives available?
Eligibility and incentive availability depend on applicable government policies, regulations, location and system requirements. These change from time to time. Confirm current eligibility before making a purchase decision — we can point you to what applies at the time of your enquiry, but we do not guarantee any incentive outcome.

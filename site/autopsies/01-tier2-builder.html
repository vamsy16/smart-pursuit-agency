A builder in a Tier-2 city is buying attention it cannot convert

A residential developer in a North Indian Tier-2 market, apartments in the ₹10–45 lakh band, running live Meta ads. I spent 25 minutes on their public footprint and found ₹0-worth of fixes that stand between that ad spend and a family walking into a site office. Published anonymised, because the point is the method, not the embarrassment.

Method: 25 minutes, by handTools: a browser, no scrapersIdentifying details removed

Why this is the most common leak in Indian real estate

Developers in smaller markets buy reach, then lose the buyer in the 40 seconds after the click: a dead landing URL, no price on the page, no RERA number, and four portal listings that disagree with each other. Media spend is the visible cost. The invisible one is the family that compared three builders in ten minutes and picked the one whose story was clean.

Total cost of the first three, if done in-house with a developer and a diligent admin: about two days of work and ₹0 in media money.

The three fixes, in order

  1. Redirect everything indexed. Search the brand name on mobile in incognito, click every result, 301 anything that 404s to the live project page. Then do it for every old campaign URL, because ads age slower than landing pages do.
  2. Put the decision data above the fold. Configuration · saleable area · price band plus charges · possession status · RERA number in the exact words filed with the regulator · payment plan · two buttons: WhatsApp and book a site visit. Copy goes underneath.
  3. Write one canonical fact sheet and correct every listing with it. One page, one truth: legal entity, RERA per phase, unit count, sizes, price bands, possession, OC status. Portals get updated to match it, and nobody publishes a bait price again.

How I'd do it, with money and 30 days

Week 1 · stop the leak

Redirects and canonical fact sheet live. Listing hygiene across every portal. Auto-reply and speed-to-lead rule (15 minutes in office hours, Sunday covered) written into the sales office, not into a PDF nobody reads.

Week 2–4 · make it measurable

A page per phase, used as the ad destination. Tracking so every enquiry has a source that can be named in a meeting. The account rebuilt around the one creative that already works, with a written cap and a stop rule. Weekly one-page report: walk-ins booked, not impressions served.

What I would not do for a business like this: pay-per-lead or ₹-per-walk-in (that turns an agency into a broker and a broker into a squeeze), promise flats sold, touch subsidy or EWS eligibility wording without the legal file, or move budget in an account without a written cap.

The method, free to steal

Twenty-five minutes, in this order, on any business that advertises: (1) the mobile brand search, (2) the ad's landing page on 4G with the keyboard closed, (3) does the page answer price, area, timeline, proof, and how to act, (4) the top three listings and whether they agree, (5) how old the newest creative and photo are, (6) what happens to an enquiry at 21:00 and on Sunday, (7) one competitor's same five answers. If three of those seven are bad, the ad budget is not the problem and more of it won't help.

One engagement.
A department's output.

06 · What stays human

Four things this company
will never automate.

Publishing this list is a positioning move, not a confession. Automation is why the price works. The human is why it is safe. Agencies that automate the left column are the ones who end up in refunds and screenshots.

Always a person

  • Money. Every invoice, refund, discount, scope concession and budget change. The system prepares, Vamsi decides.
  • Anything said to you. No message leaves this building unsent by a human. Not a complaint, not a "sorry for the delay", not a congratulations.
  • Judgement about your business. What to stop, which opportunity is worth the next ninety days, whether a number matters.
  • Bad news. You hear it from a person, fast, with a fix and a date attached.
  • Saying no. When a tactic would hurt you, or when we are the wrong agency, someone with a name tells you.

Always the system

  • Research, keyword mapping, competitor and market scans
  • First drafts, variants, briefs, calendars, file naming
  • Measuring, baselining, anomaly detection, chart-building
  • Quality scoring, checklist enforcement, SLA tracking
  • Filing, versioning, and remembering what was decided on which day
07 · Standards you can hold us to

Six promises, each one
checkable by you.

We will not promise you a ranking, a ROAS figure or a lead count. Anyone who does has priced the risk to you at zero. These six are the ones that actually decide whether an engagement works, and every one is written into the agreement.

P1

Nothing ships below 90 of 100

Truth, specificity, fit to your business, actionability, craft, honesty. An unsourced number fails it automatically. You can ask to see a score for any deliverable.

P2

Volume, itemised

The weekly quota is in your scope table. If we ship less, it is visible in the report without you having to ask. Countable beats impressive.

P3

A stop rule, written before launch

For any paid test: the spend level and the metric that kills it. If we miss two named indicators by day 90, the next ninety days of that work is reworked at our cost.

P4

Every figure traceable

Source system, date range, how it was pulled. Your baseline is frozen in week one and never moved to flatter us.

P5

Your money stays yours

Ad accounts, cards and spend in your name, funded five days ahead. We never front money for a client, and never bill through a personal account.

P6

Your records are yours

Files, credentials, history and IP transfer on full payment. On exit you get everything plus a free 90-day plan for whoever replaces us.

THE STANDARD, MEASURED THE SAME WAY EVERY TIME
90/100 minimum

Below 75, the work is not returned to the writer. It is returned to the process: the rule that failed gets written down so the same failure is not possible for the next client either. You benefit from our mistakes long after they stop happening.

Truth: every number traceable to a dated source 25
Specificity: could a competitor send this unchanged 20
Fit: uses your words, your product, your constraints 15
Actionability: next step named, owned, dated 15
Craft: formatting, tone, correct sizes 10
Honesty: assumptions and failure modes stated 10
Any unsourced number −25, fails

09 · Assumptions & out of scope

Where this model stops.
Read it before you sign.

A functional design that hides its limits is a sales deck. These are the conditions the service needs, and the things it will not do, however much you pay.

Access to analytics, search console, ad accounts, the site week 1
One decision-maker who answers within 48 hours always
Product facts, prices, policies, and what you cannot claim week 1
Weekly numbers, or standing access to pull them Mon, 30 sec

Out of scope, deliberately

24/7 community management and reply duty no
Bought followers, reviews, links, or engagement pods never
Guaranteed rankings, ROAS or lead counts never
Fronting your ad spend or payroll never
Photography, printing, influencer fees pass-through
Legal, tax or medical claims advice your professionals
Work below ₹1,00,000, however small the ask no

Each refusal is capability protection, not attitude. The 8-client ceiling, the no-black-hat rule and the floor price are what allow the things above the line to be delivered at all.

And one limit about us: we are eight clients, on purpose.

Capacity is capped at eight concurrent retainers. At five, prices for new clients rise and a start-date queue is published. At eight, retainer sales stop and we offer sprints or a waitlist. This is not scarcity theatre and it is not laziness: beyond eight, the senior person stops being in the room and becomes an operations manager, and you paid for the strategist. When you need more than one senior person can carry, we tell you honestly and, if useful, help you build the in-house team that should replace us.

If your needs are genuinely 24/7, or you require a named account director, a procurement-grade vendor register, or a support SLA measured in hours, we are the wrong company for you and a good local full-service agency will serve you better. We will say this on the first call rather than at month three.

10 · Questions clients actually ask

Answered the way we
would answer your CFO.

Is the work machine-made? Should I care?

Yes to the first, no to the second. Machine-made is how ~47 items a week exist at this price instead of six. What is human is the strategy, the judgement about your business, and accountability. You can ask what was drafted by the system on any deliverable; we will tell you. What you will never get is unaudited output, because everything passes the same numeric gate before you see it.

What happens when you are sick or on holiday?

The system continues producing from the approved calendar, your report still arrives, and emergency contact details are in your folder. Nothing about your account lives in one person's memory: every decision, credential reference and open item is in your file, which is exactly why we can promise continuity better than a team with high turnover can.

Why should I trust one person with ₹10L?

Don't trust the person; read the process. Everything is written, dated and retrievable: the scope, the weekly counts, the source of every number, the reason behind every pause. You can audit any week years later. Trust that survives an audit is worth more than trust that survives a charm offensive.

Can you start smaller and grow if it works?

Yes, and it is the honest path: ₹25,000 for the audit and the 90-day plan, or ₹1,00,000 for a thirty-day sprint on one channel. What we will not do is a discounted retainer, because a discounted engagement teaches both of us the wrong lessons about commitment.

Who owns the work?

You do, on final payment: files, accounts, creatives, copy, code, the repository. On day one you own everything we send you. There is no proprietary dashboard holding your history hostage, which is why leaving is cheap for you and why we have to earn staying every month.

How do you compare to a big agency?

You get more output per rupee, faster decisions, one throat to choke, and less of what agencies actually charge for: layers, account management, decks about work instead of work. You get fewer bodies in a room, no overnight emergency response, and no brand-production team. If the requirement is a room of bodies, hire the room.

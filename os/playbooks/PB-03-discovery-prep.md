# PB-03 · Discovery-call prep (the 1-page brief)

```
Trigger:        "prep <slug>" · any call booked within 24h (inbound lead, client review, renewal)
Inputs:         vault lead card or client folder (MANIFEST, 00-account, 06-comms, 04-analytics) ·
                os/pricing/RATES.md · os/engines/02-sales/objections.md
Output:         .vault-work/…/02-strategy/prep-call-<date>.md — ONE page, five blocks, readable in 4 minutes
SLA:            5 minutes before the call. Longer is a defect: the point is a page, not a report.
Gate:           Truth + Actionability (each block must change what Vamsi says or asks)
Owner:          R1/R7 · the call itself is 100% human
```

**The five blocks, in this order:** **Ask** (the one decision that must come out of this call, with the option
letters A/B and the recommendation) · **Show** (three things true since the last contact, each with its number
and date) · **Their words** (verbatim quote of what success means to them; the metric they used, not ours) ·
**Read** (mood from the last three exchanges, health, and what not to raise today and why) · **Log after**
(the 15-minute `log:` dictation is scheduled as part of the call, not remembered afterwards).

Rules: no slide, no deck, no "overview of the company" (they know their own company); always state the current
number even when it's unflattering; never open a price conversation with a range wider than the published card;
if the call is with a junior and no owner can be reached, the prep page says so and the offer becomes the audit.

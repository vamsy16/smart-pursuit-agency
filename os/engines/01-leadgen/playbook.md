# ENGINE 01 — Demand & Pipeline (why you will not be bored)

The founder's real problem isn't "no money to hire". It's **no predictable demand**. This engine
exists so that pipeline is a *manufactured output*, not a feeling. It runs on 4 free rails, all of
which produce assets that double as sales collateral.

## The 4 rails (and what each one is really for)

### Rail 1 — Signature Work (the trust engine) · ₹0
**"The Growth Autopsy"** — a public, named, repeatable study: we take a real niche/website problem
apart in 1,200 words + 8 slides + a 5-min Loom-style walkthrough, published weekly.
- Why: at ₹10L, nobody buys a service. They buy *the person who understood their business in 3 minutes.*
- Cadence: 1/week. System drafts, you add one opinion and record the 5-minute voiceover. 25 min of you.
- Assets: `engines/01-leadgen/autopsies/`. Each one is a lead magnet, an email, 3 carousel frames,
  a DM opener and a proposal appendix.

### Rail 2 — Search + Local compounding · ₹0
- **Programmatic-lite SEO:** 20 pages from one template per month, targeting
  "[vertical] + [city] + [problem]" and "[tool] alternative / vs" for the AI stack you actually use.
- **Google Business Profile:** 3 posts/wk, Q&A seeding (10 questions, answered by us), review-reply
  drafts inside 24h. Local is where A3 clients are, and it's where almost no premium agency bothers to show up.
- **Answer-engine visibility:** publish the same autopsy in a format Gemini/ChatGPT/Perplexity can
  cite (question-style H2s, short quotable definitions, comparison tables). Cheap today, expensive to
  retrofit later. This is the 2026 version of "rank on Google" and it costs you nothing to be early.

### Rail 3 — Warm-ish Outbound (the fastest cash) · ₹0
Target: 40/day, researched by the system, personalised by the system, **sent by you** (never a
spray tool; deliverability and dignity both survive this way).
- List building: Google Maps + directories + funded-startup lists + job boards (a company hiring
  "growth marketer" is a company with budget and pain — this is the single best free intent signal).
- Offer: not "we do marketing". It's **"I'll send you a 5-minute teardown of what's losing you money
  — if it's useful, we talk."** Reply rate is 5–10x a pitch on quality alone.
- Sequence: D0 teardown offer → D2 the actual teardown → D7 one insight from their space → D21
  "closing the file — anything I should know?" Never more than 4 touches. Respect sells at this price.
- International rail: same mechanic, LinkedIn + email, US/EU/DXB hours (see `ops/cadence.md`),
  always 3-4pm IST calls = 6-7am ET / 8:30am GMT.

### Rail 4 — Own the room (authority, not vanity)
- **Build-in-public:** you are a solo founder building a ₹10L-capable AI OS. *That story is the lead
  magnet.* Post the numbers, the mistakes, the invoices (redacted), what the system did this week.
  Nobody in your market has this story; it is an unfair advantage you already own.
- **The partner loop:** 10 conversations with complementary people (web shops, CAs, branding
  studios, CRM devs, photographers). They refer, you refer, ₹0 spend, 30% of A-tier deals.
- **One free workshop per quarter** for a real audience (chamber, startup hub, college, industry WhatsApp group).

## The pipeline maths (print this and put it on the wall)

To close **1 retainer/mo at ₹1.5L**:
`25 conversations → 12 qualified → 6 proposals → 1–2 closes.`
To reach 25 conversations: ~40 outbound/day + 1 autopsy/week + SEO compounding, and **that's all**.
This is why the engine's job is measured in *conversations booked*, not impressions, followers or
"engagement". If a rail isn't producing conversations by week 6, it gets cut — no sentiment.

## Lead magnets that qualify instead of attracting freeloaders
| Magnet | Attracts | Filter built in |
|---|---|---|
| Growth Autopsy (teardown of *their* site) | Owners | Requires them to answer the 9 intake fields to receive it |
| "Budget-to-Output" calculator (sheet) | Funded teams | Asks for spend; low spend sees a different result |
| 90-day Growth Plan template | Operators | Positions you as the one who writes the real plan |
| The Agency Price Card (public) | Serious buyers | States the ₹1L floor up front — pre-kills Tier C |

**Publish the price card.** Yes, publicly. It repels the wrong 80% in 4 seconds and makes the right
20% assume you're worth it. This is the highest-leverage free move in the entire design.

## KPIs (weekly, by D08/D12)
- New qualified conversations booked (target ≥6/wk by week 8)
- First-response time (target <5 min inbound / <2h outbound reply)
- Rails' attribution (which rail booked it) — kill the bottom rail monthly
- Autopsy → reply rate, outbound → reply rate, and **reply → call** rate
- Pipeline value in ₹ (not "leads"). Weeks of pipeline coverage ≥ 6.

## What this engine must never do
Buy followers, run engagement pods, mass-DM with a tool, publish "10 tips for social media" filler,
or chase a trend that produces no conversations. Reach is not revenue; **conversations are.**

# ENGINE 05 — Retention, Expansion & the Bench

**The economics that decide everything:** a new ₹1.5L retainer costs you 15–25 hours of founder time to
win. Keeping it costs ~4. A client who stays 18 months is worth ₹27L from one relationship. Which means
*the retention engine is the profit engine*, and at solo scale it is also the only realistic way to grow.

## Retention is a rhythm, not a feeling
| Cadence | Ritual | Who runs it |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly | Digest on time, always the same hour | R8 auto |
| Every 2 weeks | 10-minute personal call with the **decision-maker** (not the marketing manager) | You |
| Monthly | MBR with decisions and money talk | R6 drafts, you lead |
| Day 45 & Day 135 | **"Are we on track?" check-in** — the two moments where quiet cancellations happen | R8 |
| Quarterly | QBR + 90-day forward plan + expansion offer | You |
| 60 days before renewal | Auto-drafted renewal paperwork + case study of their own results | R8 + R9 |

## The early-warning system (D11 — worth more than all lead gen combined)
Signals, scored per client weekly. Any 3 amber or 1 red = `ESCALATE` to the founder within 24h.
- Client replies to digests trending down / approval turnaround >72h
- 2+ weeks with no requests from them (**danger: they've started looking elsewhere**)
- Metrics flat 6+ weeks against baseline
- Meetings skipped or delegated downward (the #1 pre-churn signal in agency data; it's about *people*, not numbers)
- Late or partial payment, new finance contact added, "how do we measure ROI exactly?" emails
- Competitor mention, new in-house job posting for a role we cover, a sudden interest in "process" and "SOW"

**Counter-move, and this is the actual play:** the moment amber appears, do **not** send a reassurance
email. Send a *new insight they didn't ask for* — one untaken opportunity, sized in money, with a 3-step
plan. Churn is usually boredom, not disappointment. Break the boredom.

## Expansion ladder (raise price by adding, never by repricing an existing account)
1. **Add a channel** — SEO → add paid, or paid → add email/CRO. (+₹0.8–1.5L/mo)
2. **Add a market** — one geography or one product line. +₹1–2L/mo
3. **Deepen the asset** — conversion programme, lifecycle automation, landing-page factory. +₹0.5–1L/mo
4. **Project upsell** — full website/funnel rebuild (₹3–12L), brand system (₹2–5L), data/tracking build (₹1–3L)
5. **Advisory retainer** — for founders who want you across the business, ₹50–1L/mo, 2 hrs, and it's the highest-margin rupee in the company.

Trigger: expansion is *offered* in the MBR when 2 consecutive months beat plan, or when a new channel is
obviously leaving money on the table. Never "if you need anything else…".

## Churn offboarding (protects future revenue)
Exit interview as a 6-question async form · final handover pack (all assets, docs, credentials,
account history, and a **free 90-day plan they can hand to whoever replaces us**) · remove access the same day ·
log the real reason in `memory/lessons.md` · stay warm; ex-clients are a referral channel and 1 in 5 comes back at a higher rate.
A graceful exit is a marketing asset. Most agencies burn it and never notice the cost.

## The Bench (your fake employees, paid per job, ₹0 fixed)
Solo capacity ceiling is 8 retainers. The Bench is what lets you say yes to the 9th, 12th and 20th without hiring.
Recruit now, before you need them: keep 8–12 vetted freelancers on file in `ops/bench/` — video editor,
graphic designer, web dev, copy editor, ad specialist, analyst, VA.

Per bench member: what they're good at, rate (₹/deliverable), turnaround, quality score from last 3 jobs,
and a warm-contact note logged every 60 days so they remember you exist.
Economics: a ₹1.5L client's 12 assets/week cost ₹18–25k on the bench → **83% margin, zero fixed risk,
instantly reversible.** The OS briefs and reviews their work; the founder only sees exceptions.
Rule: bench work still passes `quality-gate.md` ≥90. The gate is what makes a distributed team look like one brand.

## Referrals (the free, high-close-rate channel most agencies never systematise)
- Ask **at the moment of a win**, not at renewal: after a strong MBR, in the same message: "who else
  like you should be doing this?" Two named suggestions beat an open question.
- Formal: 1 month of a partner channel = 10% of first 3 months of a referred retainer, credited or
  paid, always in writing. Say it on proposals; it costs only when it works.
- Every new client: "how did you find us?" → feeds `metrics.yml`, so Engine 01's rails stay honest.

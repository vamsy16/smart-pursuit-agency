# ENGINE 02 — Sales (discovery → proposal → close)

At ₹10L, you do not "sell". You **diagnose, then offer.** The system's job is to hand you a folder so
complete that the 45-minute call feels like a consultation, not a pitch — and to assemble the proposal
in 20 minutes instead of a lost weekend.

## Stage machine (mirrors `pipeline/stages.md`)
```
LEAD → QUALIFIED → DIAGNOSED → PROPOSED → NEGOTIATING → WON → ONBOARDING
                                    ↘ PARKED (nurture, 90d)
                                    ↘ LOST (reason mandatory, feeds D13 + Engine 01)
```
Every lead sits in exactly one stage. Every stage has a **next action with a date** or it is a bug
(flagged by D05 daily).

## 1. The Discovery Call — 45 minutes, structured, no deck
**Prep the system gives you (read 4 minutes before dialling):** 1-page brief = their 9 intake answers,
what their site/funnel actually does wrong (3 concrete items), 2 competitors, what they're probably
spending, 3 questions that will decide fit, and the recommended offer tier with a one-line justification.

**Call shape (never skip 1 and 5):**
1. Frame (2 min): "This is a working session, not a pitch. If I'm not useful in 45 minutes, say so and we hang up."
2. Their story (12 min): where it's going well, where it isn't, what they've already tried, what they *think* is broken.
3. Diagnosis (10 min): you tell them what you see that they can't — the constraint, the leak, the missing asset.
4. Cost of inaction (5 min): "if nothing changes for 6 months, what does that cost?" Let them say the number.
5. The offer + ranges (10 min): present the **structure and the floor**, never a single number to react to.
6. Close on next step (6 min): audit + written proposal in 72h, and the decision meeting date. Book it on the call.

**Language rules:** talk about **the business outcome and the risk**, not deliverables. "You'll get 14
articles" is a commodity. "By month 4 you'll have a channel that sends 30–50 sales-ready enquiries
without paying for them, and here's what that's worth against your ₹2.1L average deal" is a ₹1.5L/mo conversation.

**Qualify out loud.** Say: "our retainers start at ₹1L/mo, minimum 6 months, media budget separate.
Is that workable?" — on the call, early. It costs nothing and saves a 5-hour proposal.

## 2. Proposal assembly (R7, ≤20 min, from `templates/proposal`)
Structure (8 pages, no more):
1. **What we heard** — their words, their numbers, one sentence on the constraint.
2. **The bet** — what we're doing, why this and not the obvious thing, in 5 lines.
3. **Scope table** — what's in / what's explicitly out (this page raises price more than any other).
4. **3 options** — Anchor high, land middle: e.g. Foundation ₹1.5L/mo · Growth Dept ₹2.5L/mo (recommended, "most choose this") · Market Entry ₹4L/mo. For projects: ₹10L / ₹16L / ₹25L.
5. **90-day plan with dates** — week-by-week, and what the client must supply (their laziness is your delay risk; put it in writing).
6. **Proof** — case study, or the honest "Month 1 is a paid test of the system: here's exactly what will be measured and what would make us stop."
7. **Commercials** — payment terms, advance, auto-renew, cancellation notice, ad spend ownership, validity (14 days).
8. **Next step** — one button: sign + advance, and the kickoff date.

**Never** send a proposal without: a named decision-maker, an expiry date, a payment term, and a
"what we need from you" list.

## 3. The follow-up machine (all drafted by system, sent by you — L0)
- T+0: proposal sent + a 3-line note referencing their own words from the call.
- T+1: "one thing I forgot" — a single extra insight (highest reply-rate email that exists).
- T+3: a 90-second Loom walkthrough of pages 2–4. Watch time = trust.
- T+7: a case study / a small free fix. Value, not pressure.
- T+12: "closing the file?" — ask if it's a *no*, a *not now*, or *nothing yet*. Silence is usually "nobody said no to me".
- T+21: park, and drop into the 90-day nurture (they come back — 20–30% of A-tier deals close later).

**Objections, answered once, reused forever** (`engines/02-sales/objections.md`): too expensive ·
we tried an agency · why not hire in-house · can you guarantee results · start smaller · need partner's approval ·
our market is different · we'll do it ourselves with AI.

## 4. Close & handoff (this is where trust is either kept or destroyed)
1. Signed doc + advance received **before any work starts.** No exceptions, not even for friends.
2. R8 fires the kickoff pack: welcome note, access-request checklist, first-30-days calendar, comms
   rules (how fast you reply, when, where), monthly review dates booked **now** for 6 months.
3. Founder records a 3-minute personal welcome video. Costs nothing, and it's the reason clients stay 2 years.
4. Ledger entry, folder creation, dashboards live, ticket `0001-kickoff` opened.

## Rules of the engine
- No proposal without a call. No call without the 9-field intake. No kickoff without an advance.
- Never discount. If budget is short: **reduce scope, not price.** The smallest paid thing at the floor
  (Sprint ₹1L) beats a "discounted retainer" that will resent you.
- Every LOST record gets a real reason + what we'd do differently. That file is the highest-ROI 5
  minutes in the whole system: it's how a solo operator gets smarter faster than agencies with sales teams.

# Capacity Governor

The most common death for a talented solo agency is not bad marketing. It's saying yes four times too
often. This file makes "no" a **mechanical** decision instead of a confidence problem.

## Ceiling

| Load | Retainers (at Engine 03 quota) | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | 0–4 | Normal. Sell, onboard, ship. |
| 🟡 Amber | 5–7 | Auto: **prices rise 20%** on new proposals, onboarding queue published ("starts Oct 1"), Sprint tier promoted over retainers. |
| 🔴 Red | 8 | **Retainer sales pause.** Engine 02 refuses to assemble a retainer proposal without a written founder override. Bench activated. Only Sprints + Audit, or hire. |
| ⚫ Break | 8 + bench at 90% | Everything waits. Say the waiting-list date out loud. This is a *positioning* event, not a failure — "we start in 5 weeks" is how premium actually works. |

Equivalent project math: a ₹10L project ≈ 2.5 retainer-equivalents. Track both against one budget:
**weekly delivery hours committed ≤ 22** (the rest of the 2h15m window is pipeline + approvals).

## The rules that enforce it
1. Every onboarded client adds a fixed row to `capacity.md`: their weekly quota items × your minutes.
2. D01 morning brief computes `committed delivery hours / available` — if >100% for 3 days, it escalates **before** you feel it.
3. Over-capacity work goes to the `ops/bench/` (margin-protecting, reversible) — never to your evenings.
4. The system's *first* answer at Red is **raise price**, second is **waitlist**, third is **bench**.
   Hiring a full-time employee is last, and requires 6 months of payroll in the bank + 40 hrs/week of
   supervisable work. A manager hire before that is how a ₹10L agency becomes a ₹0 agency with a salary problem.

## Why 8, not 15
8 retainers × ₹1.5–2.5L = ₹1.2–2.0 crore/month of billings from **one** person, at ~55 hrs/mo of delivery
+ approvals each. Beyond that you are no longer the senior strategist in the room — you become an
operations manager, and clients at this price point pay for the strategist. If you want 20 clients, that's
a *different business* (and a different design): the bench becomes a studio, and the OS becomes the thing
you sell to other agencies. Keep that door in mind; don't walk through it from a laptop with ₹0.

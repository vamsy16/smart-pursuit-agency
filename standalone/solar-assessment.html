Where most people start

Your electricity bill keeps coming every month

Before deciding whether solar is right for your property, there are a few things worth checking first — and most of them have nothing to do with panels.

Your electricity usage

How many units you consume each month, and how much of that usage happens during daylight hours, drives everything else.

Your available roof

Usable shade-free area, roof structure and access determine what can realistically be installed.

Your actual requirement

System size should follow your consumption — not a package someone recommended without looking at your property.

Why consider solar

What rooftop solar can offer

  • Lower electricity dependence — generate part of your own power during daylight hours.
  • Long-term savings potential — depending on usage, tariff structure and generation.
  • Better control over energy costs — a portion of your expense becomes more predictable.
  • Use of unused rooftop space — most roofs sit empty.
  • Renewable generation — electricity produced where it is consumed.
  • Potential eligibility for incentives — subject to applicable government policies and regulations.

Savings and generation depend on site conditions, consumption, tariff structure, equipment and applicable regulations. No guaranteed outcome is offered.

Questions

Common questions

Is the assessment really free?
Yes. The assessment is a no-cost, no-obligation review of your usage and property. You are under no obligation to request a quotation or proceed with an installation afterwards.
How much does a rooftop solar system cost?
Cost depends on system size, equipment selection, mounting structure and roof conditions. A meaningful figure can only be given after your usage and roof have been reviewed.
Can solar reduce my electricity bill?
Solar can reduce the electricity you draw from the grid, which may reduce your bill. How much depends on your consumption pattern, tariff structure, system size and actual generation. We do not offer guaranteed savings figures.
Is rooftop solar suitable for every property?
No. It depends on usable shade-free roof area, structure, orientation, access and roof rights. Apartments usually involve shared roof ownership, which changes what is possible.
Are government incentives available?
Eligibility and incentive availability depend on applicable government policies, regulations, location and system requirements. Confirm current eligibility before making a purchase decision.
What happens after I submit the form?
A solar specialist would normally contact you to understand your electricity usage, property and requirements, and to explain the next steps. Keeping a recent electricity bill handy makes that conversation much faster.

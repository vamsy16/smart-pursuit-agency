Solar installation

What a solar installation involves

A rooftop installation is more than mounting panels. These are the parts of the job that determine how well the system performs over its life.

Site assessment

Usable shade-free roof area, orientation, roof structure and condition, access routes and where the inverter can be placed.

System design & sizing

Sized against your electricity consumption and available area — with the panel, inverter and mounting options set out clearly.

Mounting & installation

Structure fabrication and fixing, module mounting, DC and AC wiring, earthing and lightning protection where applicable.

Approvals & metering

The applicable application, inspection and metering process for your connection type and location.

Commissioning

Testing, system handover, monitoring setup and a walkthrough of how the system behaves day to day.

Service & support

Cleaning guidance, periodic checks, warranty handling and a route to reach someone when something needs attention.

Questions

Solar installation FAQs

How long does solar installation take?
The physical installation of a typical residential system is usually completed in a matter of days once materials are on site. The full timeline is longer, because it includes assessment, design, procurement and the applicable approval and metering process, which varies by location and connection type.
How much does a rooftop solar system cost?
Cost depends on system size, panel and inverter selection, mounting structure, roof conditions and the scope of work included. A meaningful figure can only be given after your usage and roof have been assessed.
How many panels will I need?
Panel count follows system size, and system size follows your monthly consumption and usable roof area. The wattage of the modules selected then determines how many panels make up that system.
Do you handle approvals and net metering?
The applicable application, inspection and metering process forms part of the installation scope. Requirements and timelines are set by the relevant authority and vary by connection type and location.
What happens during a power cut?
A standard on-grid system shuts down during a grid outage as a safety requirement. If backup during outages matters to you, a hybrid system with battery storage is the option to discuss.
Is rooftop solar suitable for every property?
No. Suitability depends on usable shade-free roof area, structure, orientation, access and roof rights. Apartment rooftops usually involve shared ownership, which changes what is possible.
What maintenance is required after installation?
Mainly keeping panels clean, since dust reduces generation, plus periodic inspection of wiring, mounting and inverter performance.
Are government incentives available?
Eligibility and incentive availability depend on applicable government policies, regulations, location and system requirements. Confirm current eligibility before making a purchase decision.

# Agency Positioning, USP & Premium Brand-of-You

## Purpose
This is the meta-skill: how the agency itself is positioned so it never gets compared apples-to-apples with commodity agencies on a rate-card — the single biggest lever for reaching ₹10L+/month without a large team.

## When to Trigger
Agency setup, and revisited every 6-12 months or when pricing feels like it's hitting a ceiling.

## Inputs Required
`[YOUR_STRONGEST_SERVICES]`, `[YOUR_BEST_RESULTS/CASE_STUDIES so far]`, `[TARGET_CLIENT_PROFILE]`

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a positioning strategist helping me build a premium agency brand.
My strongest services: [YOUR_STRONGEST_SERVICES]. Best results so far:
[YOUR_BEST_RESULTS]. Target client: [TARGET_CLIENT_PROFILE].

1. Recommend a narrow positioning statement: "The [category] agency for
   [specific niche/problem]" — narrower than "full-service digital marketing
   agency". Explain why narrow positioning commands higher prices than broad
   positioning, with the trade-off made explicit.
2. Give me 3 proof-point formats to build credibility fast even with a short
   track record: outcome-based case studies (even 1-2 strong ones), a
   published point-of-view/framework with your name on it, and a specific
   guarantee or process no generic agency offers.
3. Draft an "About/Manifesto" statement (150 words) that would make a
   time-poor CMO think "this isn't a vendor, this is a partner" within the
   first 2 sentences.
4. Give me 3 things to deliberately NOT do that most agencies do (e.g.,
   don't have a generic "services" page listing 15 services — have a
   point of view instead) to avoid blending into the commodity market.
```

## Deliverable Template
Narrow Positioning Statement (with rationale) → 3 Proof-Point Formats → About/Manifesto Draft → "What We Deliberately Don't Do" list

## Premium & QA Notes
Run the positioning statement through the Competitor Lens: could 10 other agencies in your city claim the exact same statement? If yes, it's not differentiated enough — narrow further by industry, problem, or methodology until it's uniquely yours. Niching down feels like it limits opportunity but is what actually enables premium, ₹1.5L+/month pricing.

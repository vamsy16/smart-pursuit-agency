# Contracts, IP Ownership & Legal/Compliance Basics

## Purpose
Protect the agency and the client relationship with clean legal foundations — this is table-stakes for handling ₹10L+/month in client budgets and advertiser platform access.

## When to Trigger
Every new client contract, every freelancer/vendor agreement, and annually reviewed for updates.

## Inputs Required
`[CLIENT_NAME]`, `[SERVICES_SCOPE]`, `[CONTRACT_LENGTH]`, `[JURISDICTION]` (state/country)

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a contracts consultant (note: I will have a qualified lawyer review
before execution — draft a strong starting point) for an agency-client
agreement. Client: [CLIENT_NAME], scope: [SERVICES_SCOPE], term:
[CONTRACT_LENGTH], jurisdiction: [JURISDICTION].

Draft a Master Service Agreement outline covering:
1. Scope of Services & exclusions (what is explicitly NOT included, to
   prevent scope creep)
2. Payment terms, late payment consequences, and termination for non-payment
3. IP Ownership: who owns creative/campaign work product during vs. after
   the contract, and treatment of ad account/pixel/domain ownership
   (recommend client owns their own ad accounts/pixels always - agency
   manages, doesn't own, platform access)
4. Confidentiality & data handling (especially customer data access)
5. Termination clause: notice period, kill-fee/handover obligations
6. Limitation of liability clause (cap agency liability appropriately)
7. Non-solicitation of agency staff/freelancers by the client (optional
   clause to consider)

Flag any clause that is high-risk to skip in this industry.
```

## Deliverable Template
MSA Outline (7 sections listed above), each with a plain-English explanation of why it matters, flagged by risk-if-omitted.

## Premium & QA Notes
- **This is a drafting aid, not legal advice** — always have contracts reviewed by a qualified lawyer licensed in your jurisdiction before use, especially for liability caps and termination terms.
- Always ensure the CLIENT owns their own ad accounts, analytics properties, domains, and pixels (agency operates as an authorized manager/admin) — this protects the client (good for trust/positioning) and protects you from disputes if the relationship ends.
- Keep an NDA template ready for prospects before sharing detailed strategy/audit work in the sales process — protects your competitive-analysis IP.

# Client Onboarding, Contracts & Kickoff

## Purpose
Convert a signed deal into a smoothly running engagement in under 7 days, setting expectations that prevent scope creep and protect the relationship for 12+ months.

## When to Trigger
Immediately after contract signature, before any work begins.

## Inputs Required
- `[CLIENT_NAME]`, `[SERVICES_SCOPE]`, `[MONTHLY_RETAINER]`, `[START_DATE]`, `[POINT_OF_CONTACT]`

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as an agency operations lead onboarding a new client: [CLIENT_NAME],
retainer [MONTHLY_RETAINER]/month, scope: [SERVICES_SCOPE], starting
[START_DATE].

1. Build a 7-day onboarding checklist (Day 1 to Day 7) covering: contract/
   payment confirmation, access collection (ad accounts, analytics, CMS,
   socials, brand assets), kickoff call agenda, internal brief creation,
   and first-week deliverable.
2. Draft a Kickoff Call Agenda (45 min) with time-boxed sections.
3. Draft an "Access Request" checklist email listing every platform/credential
   needed for [SERVICES_SCOPE], with a note on secure handling (no passwords
   over email/chat — use a password manager share link).
4. Draft a "Working Together" one-pager covering: communication channels &
   response times, reporting cadence, revision policy, and escalation path.
```

## Deliverable Template
- 7-Day Onboarding Checklist (table: Day | Task | Owner)
- Kickoff Call Agenda
- Access Request Checklist (with secure-sharing instructions)
- "Working With Us" one-pager (client-facing PDF-ready doc)

## Premium & QA Notes
The "Working Together" one-pager is what separates premium agencies from freelancers — it sets boundaries (response times, revision limits, escalation path) *before* the relationship starts, in writing, so it never becomes an awkward conversation later. Always send contracts via e-signature (not "reply to confirm") — it signals professionalism and is legally cleaner (see `10-legal-compliance`).

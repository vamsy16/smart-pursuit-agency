# Referral & Partnership Channel Development

## Purpose
Cold outreach alone caps growth and keeps every deal expensive to win. At the ₹10L/month portfolio level, a referral/partnership channel typically becomes cheaper, higher-trust, and higher-close-rate than cold prospecting — this skill builds that channel deliberately instead of leaving it to chance.

## When to Trigger
Ongoing, alongside cold outreach — build this from month 1, not after cold outreach plateaus.

## Inputs Required
`[YOUR_SERVICES]`, `[TARGET_CLIENT_PROFILE]`, list of complementary service providers you already know (web dev shops, PR firms, accountants, business consultants, etc.)

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a partnerships strategist for a premium marketing agency offering
[YOUR_SERVICES] to [TARGET_CLIENT_PROFILE].

1. Partner-Type Map: list 5-6 categories of complementary (non-competing)
   service providers who already serve my target client BEFORE they need
   marketing (e.g., web dev agencies, business consultants/CAs, PR firms,
   ops/HR consultants, industry-specific SaaS vendors) — explain why each
   category is a strong referral source for this specific ICP.
2. Partner Outreach Script: a message to propose a referral relationship,
   framed around mutual client value (not "send me leads") — include a
   specific reciprocal offer (e.g., you refer clients needing web dev to them
   too).
3. Referral Fee/Incentive Structure: 2 options (one-time finder's fee vs.
   ongoing revenue share) with pros/cons of each for maintaining a
   sustainable partnership.
4. Partner Enablement One-Pager: what to give a referral partner so they can
   confidently describe what you do and to whom, without a full sales
   conversation on your behalf.
```

## Deliverable Template
Partner-Type Map (Category | Why they fit | Example targets) → Partner Outreach Script → Referral Incentive Structure Options → Partner Enablement One-Pager

## Premium & QA Notes
Never let referral fee structures create a conflict of interest against the client's best interest (e.g., don't recommend a vendor because of a kickback rather than fit) — disclose referral relationships to clients where relevant; it protects trust and is often a compliance/ethics expectation in professional services.

# Case Study & Portfolio Building (Proof-Point Engine)

## Purpose
Solves the chicken-and-egg problem: proposals and positioning skills tell you to "back every claim with proof," but a new or scaling agency may not have many results yet. This skill turns even 1-2 early wins — or process/methodology alone — into credible proof, and systematizes case-study creation as an ongoing habit, not a one-time scramble.

## When to Trigger
After every engagement hits a meaningful result (even a small one), and quarterly to refresh the portfolio.

## Inputs Required
`[CLIENT_NAME — can be anonymized if needed]`, `[STARTING_SITUATION]`, `[WHAT_WAS_DONE]`, `[RESULT_DATA]`, `[CLIENT_PERMISSION: yes/no/anonymized only]`

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a case-study writer for a premium marketing agency. Client:
[CLIENT_NAME or "a D2C skincare brand" if anonymized]. Starting situation:
[STARTING_SITUATION]. What we did: [WHAT_WAS_DONE]. Result: [RESULT_DATA].

1. Write a case study in this structure: Challenge (1 para, specific, not
   generic) → Approach (3-4 bullet actions, not a service list) → Result
   (2-3 hard numbers, with timeframe) → Client Quote (if available; if not,
   write a placeholder marked [NEEDS CLIENT QUOTE] rather than inventing one).
2. Write a 1-sentence "punchline" version of this case study for use in
   proposals/pitches (e.g., "Took [Client]'s organic traffic from X to Y in
   90 days without increasing content spend").
3. If results are modest or early-stage, give me 3 ways to present this
   credibly without exaggeration (e.g., lead with the process/methodology,
   use a % improvement even if absolute numbers are small, focus on speed of
   execution or a specific hard problem solved).
4. Give me a "No Case Study Yet" proof strategy: 3 alternative credibility
   builders to use with prospects before I have a strong portfolio (e.g., a
   free/paid audit as a live demonstration, a published point-of-view
   framework, a founder-led personal brand showing expertise in public).
```

## Deliverable Template
Full Case Study (Challenge/Approach/Result/Quote) → 1-Sentence Punchline → Modest-Results Framing Options → No-Case-Study-Yet Proof Strategy

## Premium & QA Notes
- Never fabricate a client quote or inflate a number — one exposed exaggeration destroys premium positioning permanently and is a real trust/legal risk. Mark missing elements clearly instead of inventing them.
- Always get explicit written client permission before publishing their name/logo/data — anonymized "a [industry] client" case studies are a safe default without permission.
- Build a simple habit: at the end of every month, ask "did anything happen this month worth turning into a case study?" — don't wait for a dramatic result; consistent small wins compound into a strong portfolio faster than waiting for one big story.

# Lead Generation & Prospecting

## Purpose
Build a predictable pipeline of qualified leads who can afford and value premium retainers (₹1.5L–₹5L+/month per client) — not high-volume, low-value leads.

## When to Trigger
Weekly, as a standing pipeline activity — not just "when work is slow."

## Inputs Required
- `[NICHE/VERTICAL]` (e.g., D2C skincare, B2B SaaS, real estate)
- `[ICP_REVENUE_RANGE]` (target client's annual revenue band)
- `[GEOGRAPHY]`
- `[SERVICE_FOCUS]` (which of your services you're leading with)

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a B2B growth strategist specializing in [NICHE/VERTICAL].
I run a premium digital marketing agency. My ideal client is a [ICP_REVENUE_RANGE]
company in [GEOGRAPHY] that needs [SERVICE_FOCUS].

1. Give me 5 identifiable "buying signals" I can search for on LinkedIn, job boards,
   and news sites that indicate a company in this niche is about to invest in
   marketing (e.g., just raised funding, just hired a new CMO, job posting for
   "Growth Manager", recent negative reviews about their online presence, etc.)
2. For each signal, give me the exact search query/filter to find it.
3. Write 3 cold outreach message variants (LinkedIn DM, email, and a warm-intro
   ask) that lead with a specific, researched observation about their business —
   never a generic "we help businesses grow" opener.
4. Give me a weekly prospecting cadence (how many companies/day, follow-up
   sequence, timing) realistic for one person to execute alongside client work.
```

## Deliverable Template
- Buying Signal Matrix (signal → where to find it → search query)
- 3 outreach message variants, each ≤120 words
- Weekly cadence table (Day | Activity | Volume | Time required)

## Premium & QA Notes
Never lead outreach with your service list. Lead with a specific, verifiable observation about *their* business (a page-speed issue, an outdated ad, a competitor outranking them). Run every outreach variant through the Competitor Lens in `09-qa-testing`: "Would a sharp CMO forward this to a colleague, or delete it as spam?"

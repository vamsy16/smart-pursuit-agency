# Team, Freelancer & Vendor Management

## Purpose
Scale delivery capacity beyond one person's hours without losing quality control — critical once you're running multiple ₹1.5L+/month retainers.

## When to Trigger
When capacity check (from project-management skill) signals you're near limit, or a specialized skill (video editing, design, dev) is needed.

## Inputs Required
`[TASK/ROLE_NEEDED]`, `[BUDGET_PER_TASK_OR_MONTHLY]`, `[QUALITY_BAR_EXAMPLES]`

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as an agency resourcing consultant. I need to bring on freelance/contract
help for [TASK/ROLE_NEEDED], budget [BUDGET_PER_TASK_OR_MONTHLY].

1. Write a job brief/scope doc for this role: deliverables, required skill
   proof (portfolio ask), and a paid test-task to evaluate quality before
   committing to ongoing work.
2. Give me a screening rubric (5 criteria, weighted) to evaluate applicants
   objectively rather than on gut feel alone.
3. Draft a Statement of Work / freelancer agreement template covering scope,
   payment terms, IP ownership (agency/client owns all work product), and
   confidentiality.
4. Give me a quality-control checkpoint process: how I review their output
   before it reaches a client, without becoming a bottleneck myself.
```

## Deliverable Template
Job Brief with Paid Test Task → Screening Rubric → Freelancer SOW/Agreement Template → QC Checkpoint Process

## Premium & QA Notes
Every freelancer contract must explicitly assign IP/work-product ownership to your agency (not the freelancer) so you can deliver clean ownership to clients — see `10-legal-compliance`. Never let a freelancer's output go directly to a client without your QC pass — you are the quality guarantee clients are paying premium for.

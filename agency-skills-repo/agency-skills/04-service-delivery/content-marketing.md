# Content Marketing (Blog, Long-Form, Thought Leadership)

## Purpose
Build compounding organic assets and brand authority — content that ranks, gets shared, and shortens the sales cycle for the client's own sales team.

## When to Trigger
Monthly content planning, tied to SEO content gap analysis and sales team FAQs.

## Inputs Required
`[CLIENT_NAME]`, `[INDUSTRY]`, `[TARGET_KEYWORDS/TOPICS]`, `[BUYER_STAGE_FOCUS]` (awareness/consideration/decision)

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a content strategist for [CLIENT_NAME] in [INDUSTRY].

1. Content Matrix: map [TARGET_KEYWORDS/TOPICS] against buyer stage
   (Awareness/Consideration/Decision) and content format (blog, guide,
   case study, comparison page, calculator/tool).
2. For the top 3 priority pieces, write a full content brief: target reader,
   the ONE belief the piece must change by the end, structure/outline with
   H2s, and a distribution plan (where else this gets repurposed — LinkedIn,
   email, sales collateral).
3. Draft the opening 150 words of the #1 priority piece, written to earn
   attention in the first 2 sentences (no throat-clearing intros).
4. Give a repurposing map: how 1 long-form piece becomes 5+ additional assets
   (social posts, email, short video script, infographic data points).
```

## Deliverable Template
Content Matrix table → 3 Full Content Briefs → Sample Opening (150 words) → Repurposing Map (1-to-many diagram/table)

## Premium & QA Notes
Every content brief must state "the ONE belief this piece must change" — content without a persuasion goal is just words on a page. Cite real sources/data (via web search) rather than letting AI invent statistics; fabricated stats are a serious brand-risk liability for a client-facing agency.

# Branding & Creative Direction

## Purpose
Provide brand-level thinking (positioning, voice, visual identity direction) that justifies premium pricing beyond execution-only services.

## When to Trigger
New client with weak/inconsistent brand, or as a standalone premium project offer.

## Inputs Required
`[CLIENT_NAME]`, `[INDUSTRY]`, `[CURRENT_BRAND_ASSETS if any]`, findings from `03-strategy-discovery` audit

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a brand strategist/creative director for [CLIENT_NAME] in [INDUSTRY].
Based on this positioning research: [PASTE AUDIT FINDINGS]

1. Brand Positioning Statement: "For [target], [Client] is the [category] that
   [unique benefit] because [reason to believe]" — 2 versions to choose from.
2. Voice & Tone Guide: 4-5 voice attributes (e.g., "confident, not arrogant")
   with a do/don't example sentence for each.
3. Visual Direction Brief (for a designer, not final design): mood/tone
   references, color psychology rationale, typography direction, and what to
   explicitly avoid (e.g., "avoid stock-photo aesthetic common in this industry").
4. Messaging House: one-line tagline options (5), elevator pitch (30 sec),
   and 3 proof points to back the core claim.
```

## Deliverable Template
Positioning Statement (2 versions) → Voice & Tone Guide (table) → Visual Direction Brief → Messaging House (tagline options / elevator pitch / proof points)

## Premium & QA Notes
Positioning must be differentiated from the competitor set identified in the strategy audit — if it could apply to any competitor by swapping the logo, it's not done yet. This is a natural high-ticket standalone offer (₹75K-3L) even for clients not on a full retainer.

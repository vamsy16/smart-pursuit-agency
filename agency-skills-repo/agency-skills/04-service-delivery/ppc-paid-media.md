# Paid Media (Google, Meta, LinkedIn Ads)

## Purpose
Run paid media as a profit center for the client, with transparent, defensible reporting — the #1 area where agencies lose client trust due to vague reporting.

## When to Trigger
Campaign setup, and every optimization cycle (weekly review, monthly strategy).

## Inputs Required
`[CLIENT_NAME]`, `[MONTHLY_AD_BUDGET]`, `[PLATFORM(S)]`, `[TARGET_CPA_OR_ROAS]`, `[PRODUCT/OFFER]`, `[PAST_CAMPAIGN_DATA if available]`

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a senior paid media strategist managing [MONTHLY_AD_BUDGET]/month on
[PLATFORM(S)] for [CLIENT_NAME], selling [PRODUCT/OFFER], targeting
[TARGET_CPA_OR_ROAS].

1. Campaign Architecture: recommend campaign structure (types, ad sets/groups,
   budget split %) with reasoning for each split.
2. Audience Strategy: 3 audience segments to test (cold/warm/retargeting) with
   estimated relative size and expected funnel role.
3. Ad Creative Brief: for each segment, 2 ad angle concepts (hook + core
   message + CTA) - each grounded in a specific customer pain point or desire,
   not generic "shop now" messaging.
4. Testing Plan: what variable to test first (creative/audience/offer),
   minimum spend/time needed before a valid read, and kill criteria.
5. Weekly Reporting Template: the exact metrics to report, in what order,
   with a "so what" narrative line for each (not just a raw metrics table).
```

## Deliverable Template
Campaign Architecture diagram/table → Audience Segments table → Ad Creative Briefs (2 per segment) → Testing Plan (Variable | Budget/Time Needed | Kill Criteria) → Weekly Report Template

## Premium & QA Notes
- Never let AI generate CPC/CPM/conversion rate benchmarks and present them as fact to a client — pull real numbers from the ad platform or verified industry benchmark reports.
- Every ad creative must pass the Psychological Lens: specificity beats cleverness (a specific number/outcome outperforms a clever pun almost every time).
- Report ROAS/CPA net of ad spend AND your fee — clients judge you on total ROI, not platform-reported numbers alone.

# Influencer & Creator Marketing

## Purpose
Run influencer campaigns with measurable ROI and vetted creators — differentiate from agencies that just "send products to influencers."

## When to Trigger
Campaign planning for awareness/consideration-stage goals, or client-requested influencer push.

## Inputs Required
`[CLIENT_NAME]`, `[PRODUCT/OFFER]`, `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]`, `[BUDGET]`, `[PLATFORM_FOCUS]`

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as an influencer marketing strategist for [CLIENT_NAME], budget [BUDGET],
platform [PLATFORM_FOCUS], audience [TARGET_AUDIENCE].

1. Creator Tiering Strategy: recommend a mix across nano/micro/mid/macro
   creators with budget allocation % and the specific role each tier plays
   (reach vs. trust vs. conversion).
2. Vetting Checklist: red flags for fake engagement/followers, audience-fit
   checks, and past brand-safety concerns to screen for.
3. Brief Template for creators: campaign goal, key message (not scripted
   lines — creators' authentic voice converts better), required disclosures
   (ad/sponsored per platform policy), deliverables, usage rights terms.
4. Performance Tracking Plan: UTM/promo code structure per creator, and
   the metrics that separate "impressions vanity" from real business impact.
```

## Deliverable Template
Creator Tier Allocation table → Vetting Checklist → Creator Brief Template → Tracking Plan (UTM/code structure)

## Premium & QA Notes
Always require FTC/ASCI-compliant disclosure language in briefs — this is a legal/compliance requirement in India (ASCI influencer guidelines), not optional. Negotiate usage rights for the client to repurpose top-performing creator content into paid ads.

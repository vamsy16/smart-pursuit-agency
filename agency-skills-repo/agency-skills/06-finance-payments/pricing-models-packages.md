# Pricing Models & Service Packaging

## Purpose
Design pricing that supports a ₹10L+/month portfolio (3-6 retainers, not 20 small clients) with margins that survive taxes, tools, and freelancer costs.

## When to Trigger
Agency setup, and annual pricing review.

## Inputs Required
`[SERVICES_OFFERED]`, `[TARGET_MONTHLY_REVENUE]`, `[ESTIMATED_MONTHLY_COSTS]` (tools, freelancers, etc.)

## Execution Prompt
```
Act as a pricing strategist for a premium digital marketing agency offering
[SERVICES_OFFERED], targeting [TARGET_MONTHLY_REVENUE]/month with estimated
costs of [ESTIMATED_MONTHLY_COSTS]/month.

1. Recommend a packaging structure: 3 tiers (e.g., Growth / Scale / Enterprise)
   with what's included in each, and a suggested price range for each tier
   appropriate for the Indian mid-market/enterprise segment.
2. Recommend a hybrid pricing model: base retainer + performance bonus
   structure (e.g., % of ad spend managed, or bonus tied to agreed KPIs) —
   explain when performance components make sense vs. when to avoid them.
3. Reverse-engineer: how many clients at what average retainer size are
   needed to hit [TARGET_MONTHLY_REVENUE], at 2-3 different portfolio mixes
   (e.g., "3 large + 2 small" vs "5 medium").
4. Give me 3 principles for when and how to raise prices with existing
   clients without triggering churn.
```

## Deliverable Template
3-Tier Package Table (Tier | Included Services | Price Range | Ideal Client Profile) → Hybrid Pricing Model Explanation → Portfolio Math (mix scenarios to hit revenue target) → Price-Increase Playbook

## Premium & QA Notes
Price to reflect the OUTCOME and strategic value delivered, not hours worked — a ₹10L/month portfolio requires positioning as a strategic partner (see `08-agency-positioning`), not hourly/task-based billing, which caps income to your available hours.
